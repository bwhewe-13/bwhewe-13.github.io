Ben Whewell

AI/ML Scientist at Los Alamos National Laboratory

I work on neutron transport, nuclear data, and machine learning. Most of my research is about making deterministic transport solvers faster and more accurate: reinforcement learning for multigroup energy grid optimization, hybrid numerical methods, and solution verification. I also build the ML tooling around that work, from training pipelines for simulation data to retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) tools for technical documents.

I finished my PhD in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of Notre Dame in January 2025. Before graduate school I spent three years as a boilers and turbines operator at a power station in Connecticut.

Research

  • Reinforcement learning for energy grids

    Training RL agents to choose multigroup energy structures for criticality problems.

  • Collision-based hybrid method

    Splitting the transport solve into uncollided and collided parts that use different resolutions.

  • Solution verification

    Estimating discretization error in transport solutions without running a mesh refinement study.

  • ML for nuclear data reduction

    Replacing large scattering and fission matrices with neural network surrogates.
